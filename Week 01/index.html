ITP Pcom Project Process-Lighting Box

An HTML explainer about Joy process for making ITP PComp project

1. Review the Assignment

First, Joy reads the assignment details and requirements to understand what she needs to complete.

2. Brainstorm Ideas

She thinks about topics that interest her and how they could fit the assignment.

She looks online for inspiration.
She often uses Pinterest to find visual references.
These references help her imagine what the project could look like and whether it matches her ideas.

3. Make a Timeline

After gathering inspiration, she creates a timeline and schedule to help her finish the project on time.

4. Research the Technical Requirements

Next, she researches the technical side of the project. She identifies what she needs to develop her idea and meet the assignment requirements:

Materials
Electronic components

5. Collect Components and Test a Prototype

Once she knows what she needs, she gathers the components and builds a simple prototype on a breadboard to test them.

6. Sketch and Model the Form

For a project such as a box that lights up, she starts by sketching its appearance. She then uses Fusion to create a digital model and develop the design.

7. Gather Materials and Build

She buys materials online or collects them from nearby sources.

Using her model as a guide, she builds the physical structure through methods such as:

3D printing
Woodworking
Laser cutting

8. Fit the Electronics Inside

After building the structure, she works out how to arrange the electronic components so they fit inside the box.

9. Photograph the Finished Project

When the project is complete, she photographs it against a white background to document the final result.

Documentation Throughout the Process

Joy also documents her work throughout the making process. At the end, she brings the process notes and final documentation together on her blog.
